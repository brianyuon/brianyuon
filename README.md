### 📁 Featured Projects

**🎮 [The Golden Era of Video Games (1995–2015)](https://github.com/brianyuon/video-game-sales-analysis)**
Analyzed 7,744 titles to see whether critical acclaim actually drove sales. Found that critically acclaimed games sold ~3x more than well-reviewed ones — but Nintendo still out-sold everyone despite not having the top scores.
[🔗 Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/TheGoldenEraofVideoGamesDidCriticsandPlayersAgree1995-2015/SalesCriticalAcclaim?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

**🎬 [Netflix Content Strategy Analysis (1990s–2020s)](https://github.com/brianyuon/netflix-content-strategy-analysis)**
Examined 5,835 titles to test if Netflix's rapid catalog growth came at the cost of quality. Found average IMDB scores declined as the platform scaled — quantity over quality during its biggest growth years.
[🔗 Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/NetflixContentStrategy_17806167828090/NetflixContentStrategy?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)
